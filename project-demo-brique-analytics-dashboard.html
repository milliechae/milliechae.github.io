BRIQUE Analytics

A no-code workflow builder that enables enterprise analysts to visually connect, process, and manage complex data without writing a single line of code.

I designed BRIQUE Analytics, a workflow-based data platform used by enterprise clients including Samsung and SK hynix. It enables data analysts to visually build complex data workflows without coding, monitor execution status and errors, and manage the entire process independently.

PAIN POINT

Complex data workflows were difficult to build, understand, and manage.

As data workflows became more complex, analysts had difficulty configuring each step, understanding how data moved through the process, and identifying where problems occurred.

  • Difficult to build and modify
    As workflows grew more complex, configuring new processes or modifying existing ones became increasingly difficult.
  • Hard to understand the overall flow
    It was difficult to see how each step was connected and understand how data moved from one stage to the next.
  • Limited visibility into errors
    When something went wrong, analysts had difficulty identifying where the issue occurred and which parts of the workflow were affected.
✦ DESIGN CHALLENGE

How might we simplify complex data workflows for analysts?

The goal was to turn complex data logic into a visual, intuitive workflow, making it easier to configure each step, understand the overall flow, and identify issues when they occur.

RESEARCH

Understanding how analysts work with complex data.

I spoke with analysts to understand how they prepared, transformed, and analyzed data, and where friction occurred throughout the process.

01

Repetitive tasks slowed down the workflow.

Analysts repeatedly cleaned, filtered, and joined data, often rebuilding similar processes from scratch.

02

Errors were difficult to trace.

When something went wrong, it was difficult to identify where the issue occurred and understand how it affected the rest of the workflow.

03

The overall process lacked visibility.

As workflows became more complex, analysts struggled to understand each step and inspect intermediate results.

✦ DESIGN HYPOTHESIS

Three principles shaped the experience.

Research findings were translated into three assumptions that guided our design decisions before moving into interface exploration.

01  Visualize the workflow

Make complex processes easier to understand.

02  Make issues visible

Surface status and errors where they happen.

03  Make repeated work reusable

Turn frequently used steps into reusable components.

Design Solution

We turned complex data processes into a visual workflow, making it easier to build, understand, and manage each step in one place.

A visual canvas for complex workflows.

Analysts can connect and configure each step visually, making the overall data flow easier to understand and manage.

See what's happening at every step.

Execution status and intermediate results are visible within the workflow, helping analysts identify where issues occur.

Build once, reuse when needed.

Frequently used steps can be saved as reusable components, reducing the need to rebuild the same workflows from scratch.

Impact

A clearer, more manageable way to work with complex data.

By bringing workflow creation, monitoring, and reusable components into one visual experience, BRIQUE Analytics made complex data processes easier to understand and manage.

Reflection

Visibility mattered as much as simplicity.

Simplifying how workflows were built was only part of the challenge. Making each step visible helped analysts understand what was happening throughout the process and identify issues with greater clarity.
